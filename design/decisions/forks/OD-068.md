# OD-068. Should rollback reverify a retired generation or switch to one kept current?

## The fork

**Judgment.** The registry leaves a choice between fewer steady writes and instant rollback. The row calls that a value trade no evidence weighs, so only the owner chooses it. [design/decisions/register.json:2672]

## The options

### A. Keep retired generations unwritten and reverify before rollback

**The corpus shows.** The Spec keeps retired rows unchanged for a default seven days and reopens them as `verifying`. [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:33] [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:45] The reader excludes retired generations from the writable list. [src/read-model/generations.ts:60]
**It costs.** **Judgment.** Rollback needs a verify pass before the old generation serves again. [design/specs/application/rebuild.sdp.md:84]

### B. Keep writing retired generations throughout the rollback period

**The corpus shows.** The open question names write-through, with rollback in one write and a third point read and write per read model per command for seven days. [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:33] Those are intended costs, not measured behavior. The rebuild Spec's outcome promises cutover and rollback in one write each, while its rollback rule, option A, reopens, verifies and then switches. [design/specs/application/rebuild.sdp.md:45] [design/specs/application/rebuild.sdp.md:84]
**It costs.** **Judgment.** Every live command pays the extra retired-generation maintenance during the period. [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:33]

## What only the owner decides

Does the product need instant rollback enough to pay for write-through? If it does, must that hold throughout the seven-day default period? Seven days is the Spec's overridable retention default, not a new period chosen here. [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:79]

## The lean, and the case against it

**Lean.** Keep the retired generation unwritten and the rollback that reopens it as `verifying` with one verify pass, the cheaper steady path. The row records no confidence word. [design/decisions/register.json:2672] The row holds `do` only; the three lines below are the drafter's.
**Rests on.** The registry contract and rebuild choose reverified rollback, while the reader writes only active and building/verifying/verified generations. [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:45] [design/specs/application/rebuild.sdp.md:84] [src/read-model/generations.ts:91]
**Against it.** **Judgment.** If instant rollback is a product need, waiting for a verify pass loses the value that write-through buys. [design/specs/application/generation-registry.sdp.md:33]
**What would settle it.** The owner's word on instant rollback against steady read and write cost, the trade named in the row's reason. [design/decisions/register.json:2672]
