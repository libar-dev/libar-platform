## The owner's answer

The write-pause Spec states no acceptable length. Before a paused rebuild starts, its length is estimated as the sum the lean names, backfill batches times batch time plus verify batches times batch time, and the number comes from the first paused rebuild that is measured on the native backend. Whether that length fires the doc's trigger for an online history rebuild is asked of the owner then, with the number.
