## The options

### A. Keep one document per stream and the provisional 100-line maximum

**The corpus shows.** The default is one document within a budget, with a derived mapping above it. [design/specs/kernel/state-document-mapping.sdp.md:36] Orders declares a single mapping at 256 KiB, Inventory at 16 KiB. [example/convex/orders/streams.ts:42] [example/convex/inventory/streams.ts:35]
**It costs.** **Judgment.** The maximum stays provisional; state that outgrows its budget needs a different representation. [design/specs/kernel/state-document-mapping.sdp.md:22]

### B. Rule 100 as supported and keep the single-document default

**The corpus shows.** The row's lean proposes 100 as the measured configuration under the 256 KiB budget. [design/decisions/register.json:5069] The native test asserts a 100-line applied command and its budget, but it is not a record of a run. [tests/native/first-experiment.order-of-max-lines.test.ts:85] [tests/native/first-experiment-steps.ts:330]
**It costs.** **Judgment.** The measurement size becomes a promise chosen by the owner.

### C. Choose another maximum and keep the single-document default

**The corpus shows.** The declaration bounds an order at 100 lines. [example/convex/ordering.ts:17] A maximum that does not fit the document budget requires a budget or mapping change. [design/specs/kernel/state-document-mapping.sdp.md:22]
**It costs.** **Judgment.** A different maximum needs matching declaration bounds, then a measurement at that size.

### D. Make a derived mapping the default

**The corpus shows.** The Spec names always-derived mapping as an alternative and rejects its extra reads and writes for small streams. [design/specs/kernel/state-document-mapping.sdp.md:35]
**It costs.** **Judgment.** A derived mapping adds parts and one read per part on load. [design/specs/kernel/state-document-mapping.sdp.md:43]

## The owner's answer

One answer for the largest order: 100 lines is the largest order `PlaceOrder` of the example supports, and the bound its declaration carries. It is the example's promise and no platform limit: a command's declaration carries its own bound, chosen by its author under the adapter's stream and byte ceilings. One document per stream stays the default mapping, with the derived mapping for state that outgrows its budget. The Specs stop calling 100 provisional.
