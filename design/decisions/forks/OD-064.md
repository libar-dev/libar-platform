## What only the owner decides

Should 100 remain only a measurement size, or become the supported maximum? At 1 and 10 lines the experiment has separate datasets; at 100 it tests the placeholder; 256 is the operation's count ceiling under the stated stock budget, not a chosen supported size. [design/specs/application/first-experiment.sdp.md:107] [design/specs/context/batch-shaped-api.sdp.md:47]

## The lean, and the case against it

**Lean.** Measure at the provisional 100 and hand the owner the per-line counts, with the design's own bound of 256 streams per call at the 16 KiB stock budget as the ceiling the owner chooses under [design/specs/application/first-experiment.sdp.md:112] [design/README.md:204]. Confidence: unknown. [design/decisions/register.json:2491]
**Rests on.** The adapter bounds both planned stream count and the sum of their declared budgets. [src/context/adapter.ts:270] [src/context/adapter.ts:277]
**Against it.** **Judgment.** A stream ceiling is not a complete command budget, so the recorded ceiling must not be sold as a supported order size. [design/specs/application/first-experiment.sdp.md:112]
**What would settle it.** S3's measurement at 1, 10 and 100 lines on the native backend, reported per line, then the owner's choice; the owner may set it before the run, as the lean says; its STATE.md:139 pointer is now a heading and does not support that request. [design/decisions/register.json:2491]
