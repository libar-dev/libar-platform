## The owner's answer

Stock state keeps totals only. `release` takes the quantities from the cancelled order's own lines, in the same mutation as the order's `cancel`, with Orders called first (OD-074). The stock decider rejects a release larger than the allocated total. A second cancel of one order is rejected by the order's own state, so no stock item needs to remember an order.
